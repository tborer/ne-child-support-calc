How It Works

  • Enter each parent's gross monthly income from all sources.
  • Select custody arrangement and parenting-time overnights.
  • Deductions reduce gross income to arrive at net income per parent.
  • The Nebraska Schedule of Basic Support Obligations is applied to combined net income.
  • Health insurance and child care expenses are allocated proportionally.
